About ATS Scorer - Your Free Resume ATS Checker

We built ATS Scorer because we noticed a massive problem in the modern job search. Highly qualified candidates were being rejected simply because their resumes didn't pass the initial screening of an Applicant Tracking System. We decided to create a completely free ATS scorer to level the playing field and help job seekers optimize their resumes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help job seekers bypass outdated recruitment software and get their resumes into the hands of real human beings. We do this by simulating the exact parsing errors and logical checkpoints that legacy ATS systems apply to PDF and DOCX files. By using our tool, you can see if your ATS-friendly resume is actually friendly enough to pass automated screening.

Why "Score"?

Every system from Workday to Taleo functions slightly differently, but they all share common flaws: they struggle with complex formatting like columns, headers, tables, and images. Our ATS compatibility score highlights these structural weaknesses in your document, giving you an at-a-glance read on what needs to be fixed. Learn about keywords that improve your ATS score and the best resume format for ATS systems.

How Our ATS Checker Works

Our free ATS checker analyzes your resume using the same logic that automated applicant tracking systems use:

  • Text Extraction: We simulate how ATS systems parse and extract text from your document
  • Readability Analysis: We check for formatting issues that break ATS parsing
  • Structure Evaluation: We verify that your resume has the right sections and layout
  • Keyword Matching: We identify which keywords you're using effectively
  • Scoring: We provide a 0-100 ATS score showing your overall compatibility

The Problem With ATS Systems

Most companies use Applicant Tracking Systems built in the early 2000s. These systems are primitive by today's standards—they can't read design, understand context, or make intelligent inferences. Yet they filter millions of resumes every year.

Our free ATS scorer reveals exactly what these systems see. By understanding their limitations, you can optimize your resume to get past the initial automated screening and reach actual hiring managers. Learn how to beat ATS screening.
