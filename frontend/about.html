About ATS Scorer

We built ATS Scorer because we noticed a massive problem in the modern job search. Highly qualified candidates were being rejected simply because their resumes didn't pass the initial screening of an Applicant Tracking System. We decided to create a completely free tool to level the playing field.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help job seekers bypass outdated recruitment software and get their resumes into the hands of real human beings. We do this by simulating the exact parsing errors and logical checkpoints that legacy ATS systems apply to PDF and DOCX files. By using our tool, you can see if your ATS friendly resume is actually friendly enough.

Why "Score"?

Every system from Workday to Taleo functions slightly differently, but they all share common flaws: they struggle with complex formatting like columns, headers, tables, and images. Our ATS compatibility score highlights these structural weaknesses in your document, giving you an at-a-glance read on what needs to be fixed.
