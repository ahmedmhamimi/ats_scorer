What is an Applicant Tracking System (ATS)?

An Applicant Tracking System is software used by employers to collect, sort, scan, and rank job applications. Many popular platforms like Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, and Lever utilize parsing technology that strips resumes of formatting and searches for text content, headings, and keywords. Learn more about what ATS systems are and how they work. If your resume employs complex formatting—such as tables, multi-columns, graphics, or unusual fonts—the ATS might fail to extract vital information, resulting in an automatic rejection or a poor ATS score.
