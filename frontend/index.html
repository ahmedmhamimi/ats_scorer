How ATS Machines Actually Work

We simulate the behavior of applicant tracking systems from the early 2000s — still used by 75%+ of Fortune 500 companies.

What they can't read

Images, columns, tables, text boxes, headers/footers, PDFs with embedded graphics, special fonts, and smart punctuation.

How they parse

Top to bottom, left to right, plain text only. They look for exact section headers and keyword matches. No context, no inference.

What scores you higher

Single-column layout, plain text, standard section headers, parseable email/phone, no tables or images, safe fonts.
